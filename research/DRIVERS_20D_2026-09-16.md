# 20-Day Drivers: research log (2026-09-16)

**Question.** An outside proposal, the "Core Gold Signal", scored gold's macro direction from two
inputs: 50% the 20-trading-day change in the 10Y TIPS real yield and 50% the 20-day change in the
dollar, each scaled by its own volatility to -100..+100. It had labels from STRONG BULL to STRONG
BEAR and a manually set Confidence NORMAL/LOW flag. Is it a signal, and should it replace the
Dollar (DXY / HMAI) tab?

**Verdict.** It is a good nowcast with no forecasting power, the same finding that retired the v1
forward signal (research/README.md). It ships as the **20 Days** tab, framed as "what real yields
and the dollar did to gold over the last 20 trading days" and never as a direction call. The Gold
Index and the Forward Signal are unchanged. The HMAI engine behind the Dollar tab is removed.

## 2. The proposal's other claims, checked

- **Real beats nominal: confirmed.** Same-window Pearson of 20-day gold vs each 20-day change:
  DFII10 -0.45, DGS10 -0.31. Real is stronger in every era (-0.45 / -0.45 / -0.62 / -0.46 vs
  -0.28 / -0.40 / -0.33 / -0.31).
- **Broad dollar vs DXY are equivalent** (-0.41 vs -0.42). DTWEXBGS publishes weekly, up to a week
  stale inside a 20-day window, so the app uses DXY, which it already fetches daily with no key.
- **Quoted figures verified against FRED and LBMA.** DFII10 2.41 (Aug 14) to 2.60 (Sep 14);
  DTWEXBGS 118.90 (Aug 14) to 118.21 (Sep 11); gold +13.3% in August (LBMA $4,026.60 on Jul 31 to
  $4,562.75 on Aug 28).
- **"Confidence NORMAL/LOW" was dropped.** Its triggers (geopolitics, crises, ETF flows) have no data
  source in the app. The measurable stand-ins carry no forward information:
  - **H1, change gap** (gold's 20-day move minus the macro-implied move, expanding-window OLS): next
    20d +0.00 [-0.13, +0.13], next 63d +0.07 [-0.06, +0.21]. The sign flips by era (-0.19 / -0.22
    / +0.30 / +0.10).
  - **H2, WGC-style level fair value** (log gold on DFII10 and log broad dollar, rolling 36
    months): next 63d +0.19 [-0.03, +0.42], unstable by era (+0.43 / -0.00 / +0.16 / +0.08). Gold
    was +52.5% "rich" on 2025-10-31 and then gained 20.4% in 63 days.
  - **H3, trailing fit**: trailing-2y tracking vs next-1y tracking, corr -0.26 (n=17). Tracking
    itself is dependable, positive in 16 of 17 yearly blocks.

  What shipped instead is a descriptive breakdown: gold's move = real-yield part + dollar part +
  everything else.

## 3. The shipped spec (`GoldDriversEngine`) and its own numbers

Inputs are the app's own: GLD and DX-Y.NYB from Yahoo, DFII10 from FRED.

- **Legs.** Each series is used on its own observation spine. The 20-obs change (pct pts; % for
  DXY) is divided by the sample std of its last 252 such changes (at least 126), and the leg
  scores -100 x clamp(z/2, -1, 1).
- **Headline.** The mean of the available legs, read as of GLD's last session.
- **Labels.** >=70 STRONG TAILWIND · >=30 TAILWIND · >-30 MIXED · >-70 HEADWIND · else STRONG HEADWIND.

2007 to 2026-07-02, n=246 non-overlapping: same window **+0.51** (eras +0.51 / +0.38 / +0.67 /
+0.57), next 20 days **+0.06**, next 63 days **-0.01**.

**Breakdown betas.** Regression of GLD's 20-day % change on the DFII10 change (pp) and the DXY
change (%), 2007-2026 daily overlapping, with intercept: **-6.60 and -0.76**, drift +0.93%,
R² 0.29.
- Robustness: non-overlapping -6.49 / -0.70; phase offsets -6.2 to -7.4 / -0.73 to -0.92.
- By era: -6.11 / -3.61 / -17.70 / -6.96 and -0.71 / -0.70 / -0.98 / -0.56.
- Shipped as -6.6 and -0.76.
- Whenever the headline is not MIXED, the macro part of the breakdown has the same sign as the
  headline (100% of days).

**Engine parity.** `EngineHistoryDumpTest.dumpDriversHistory` vs `drivers_replica.py`, 5,438 GLD
days: max |diff| 1.1e-5, 0 availability mismatches.

A replica run on the fresh FRED feed reads -60 for 2026-07-02 where the research cache reads -72.
The cache was fetched 07-03, before DFII10's 07-02 print was out. Both sources drop FRED's
holiday gaps the same way, so the spines match.
